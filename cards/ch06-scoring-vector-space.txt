What is the bag of words model?	A document is represented by which terms occur and how often, discarding word order. "Mary is quicker than John" and "John is quicker than Mary" have identical representations.
What is term frequency (tf)?	The number of occurrences of term t in document d. Raw tf overstates relevance: a document with 10 occurrences is not 10 times more relevant than one with 1.
What is log frequency weighting, and why use it?	w = 1 + log(tf) if tf > 0, else 0. It compresses raw counts so the tenth occurrence adds far less than the second — the same saturation idea BM25 formalizes with k1.
Document frequency (df) vs collection frequency (cf)	df = how many documents contain the term. cf = total occurrences across the collection. df is the better discriminator: "insurance" and "try" can have similar cf but very different df.
What is IDF?	idf(t) = log(N/df(t)). The weight derived from document frequency — rare terms score high, ubiquitous terms score ~0.
What is log(N/df) for a term appearing in every document?	log(N/N) = log(1) = 0. A term in every document has zero discriminating power.
Does the base of the logarithm in IDF matter for ranking?	No. Changing base multiplies every IDF by the same constant, and scaling all scores identically leaves the ordering unchanged.
Does IDF affect one-term queries?	No. With a single term, every candidate document is scaled by the same IDF, so the ranking is unchanged. IDF only matters when terms compete.
What is tf-idf, and when is it highest?	w = tf-weight x idf. Highest when a term occurs many times within a small number of documents; lowest when the term appears in nearly all documents.
What is the vector space model?	Documents and queries are vectors in |V|-dimensional term space, one axis per vocabulary term. Similarity becomes a geometric relation between vectors.
Why not use Euclidean distance between document vectors?	It is large for vectors of differing length. A document concatenated with itself has nearly identical content but sits far away in Euclidean terms.
What is cosine similarity and what does it measure?	The cosine of the angle between two vectors: the dot product of their length-normalized forms. It compares direction — the relative mix of terms — while ignoring magnitude.
Why does length normalization matter?	It stops long documents from dominating purely because they contain more of everything. In the vector space model it is Euclidean (L2) normalization; in BM25 it is division by avgdl.
What is a length-normalized vector?	A vector divided by its L2 norm, giving unit length. Two documents with the same term proportions become identical after normalization.
What is SMART notation?	A triple-letter code for the weighting scheme, written ddd.qqq — document scheme and query scheme. Each triple gives term frequency, document frequency, and normalization. lnc.ltc is the standard default.
What does lnc.ltc mean?	Documents: logarithmic tf, no idf, cosine normalization. Queries: logarithmic tf, idf, cosine normalization. IDF is applied on the query side only, avoiding double-counting.
What is the basic cosine-ranking algorithm?	Compute query term weights; for each query term, walk its postings list accumulating weighted scores per document; divide each document's score by its length; return the top K by heap.
Why use a heap rather than a full sort for the top K?	Selecting the top K from N candidates costs O(N log K) with a heap versus O(N log N) for a full sort, and K is typically tiny compared to N.
